# Produce review

Turn supplied summary and risk artifacts into a concise review. Include only
evidence they contain and turn unresolved uncertainty into a clear follow-up.
Make each follow-up self-contained by naming the specific change and the
evidence requested in the same item.

See [review-writing context](references/context.md).
