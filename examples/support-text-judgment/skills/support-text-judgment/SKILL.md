# Support text judgment

Use only the support evidence supplied for the request. Keep these categories
separate:

- observed facts, such as a recorded gateway event;
- attributed statements, such as a customer's report or an agent's note; and
- unresolved facts that the evidence does not establish.

Write a concise response or handoff that preserves those distinctions. If the
evidence does not confirm a refund, charge, delivery, or other external state,
say so plainly, identify the next check or owner, and do not invent a status or
promise that a mutation completed.
