# The SkillRoll principles

- Simplicity is the basis for adoption
- Complexity should come from composition, not inflation
- Skill behavior has an inherent trust problem
- Evals provide trust
- A skill should do one thing well
- An eval should test one behavior well
- Good skills separate prompts, scripts, and knowledge
- Prompts are evaluated
- Scripts are tested
- Context is not evlauated or tested.
- Artifacts should always be simple and readable.

- Principles change. When they no longer do, change them openly.
